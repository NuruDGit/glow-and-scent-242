Returns & Refunds

Last updated: June 2026

Our Commitment

We want you to love every fragrance you purchase from Glow & Scent 242. If something isn't right, please contact us and we'll do our best to make it right.

1. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a product that is damaged, defective, or not what you ordered, we will offer a full refund or exchange at no cost to you. Please:

  • Contact us via WhatsApp at +1 (242) 455-1882 within 48 hours of receiving your order
  • Send a photo of the damaged or incorrect item
  • We will arrange collection and replacement or refund within 3 business days

2. Change of Mind Returns

We accept returns on unopened, unused products in their original sealed packaging within 7 days of delivery. To be eligible:

  • The product must be unused and in its original sealed condition
  • Original packaging, box and any accessories must be included
  • You must contact us on WhatsApp before returning any item

Once we have confirmed your return is eligible, we will arrange collection from your Nassau address. Return delivery is free of charge.

3. Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, we are unable to accept returns on:

  • Any fragrance or perfume oil that has been opened or used
  • Items that have been damaged through misuse after delivery
  • Sale or discounted items (unless faulty)

4. Refund Process

Once we have received and inspected your return, we will notify you of the outcome via WhatsApp. Approved refunds will be processed within 3–5 business days via:

  • The same payment method used for the original order, or
  • Store credit, at your preference

5. How to Start a Return

All returns must be initiated via WhatsApp — please do not send items back without contacting us first.
