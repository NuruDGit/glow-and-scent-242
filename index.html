Our Story

The Art of Arabian Perfumery, Island Born

Glow & Scent 242 was born from a simple belief: the people of The Bahamas deserve fragrances as rich and radiant as the islands themselves. We travel to the souks and ateliers of the United Arab Emirates to hand-select every bottle — oud, amber, musk and rose, composed by the region's most celebrated houses.

From long-lasting eau de parfum to traditional alcohol-free attar oils, every scent we carry is 100% authentic, imported directly, and chosen to flourish in our warm island air.

Quick Help

Find Answers to Common Questions

Are your fragrances 100% authentic?

Yes — every bottle is imported directly from authorised distributors in the United Arab Emirates, factory-sealed and verified. We carry celebrated houses like Lattafa, Asdaaf, Afnan, Al Haramain and Armaf.

How do I place an order?

Add your fragrances to the bag and tap Checkout via WhatsApp — your order arrives to us as a message, and we confirm everything with you in chat. You can also message us directly at +1 (242) 455-1882.

Do you deliver across The Bahamas?

We deliver across Nassau — free on orders over B$100 — and ship to the Family Islands. Delivery details are confirmed over WhatsApp when you order.

What payment options do you accept?

We confirm payment options with you over WhatsApp when your order is placed — quick, simple and secure.

How long do Arabian fragrances last?

Arabian perfumery is famous for its strength. Our eau de parfum are highly concentrated and typically last 6–12 hours on skin — often longer on clothing.

What's the difference between EDP, EDT and perfume oils?

Eau de Parfum (EDP) is more concentrated and longer-lasting than Eau de Toilette (EDT). Attar perfume oils are the most concentrated of all — alcohol-free, applied by the drop, and a beautiful traditional way to wear fragrance.

How should I apply perfume for the best effect?

Spray on pulse points — wrists, neck, behind the ears — ideally on moisturised skin straight after a shower. Don't rub your wrists together; let the scent settle naturally.

Can you help me choose a fragrance?

That's our favourite part. Tell us what you love — sweet, smoky, fresh or floral — over WhatsApp and we'll match you to your perfect scent.
