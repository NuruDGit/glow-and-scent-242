Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who We Are

Glow & Scent 242 is a fragrance boutique based in Nassau, The Bahamas, importing and selling authentic luxury Arabian perfumes and perfume oils. You can contact us at:

  • WhatsApp: +1 (242) 455-1882
  • Instagram: @glowandscent242

2. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Your delivery address in The Bahamas
  • Your order details and preferences

We do not operate a formal account system. All ordering is handled via WhatsApp conversation, so the information you share is limited to what you choose to send us directly.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely to:

  • Process and fulfil your order
  • Coordinate delivery to your address
  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Send you order confirmations and updates via WhatsApp

We will only contact you about your order unless you explicitly ask to receive product updates or promotions.

4. How We Store Your Information

Your information is held within WhatsApp conversations and is subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy (Meta Platforms, Inc.). We do not store your personal data in any external database or CRM system. Order records are kept for our internal accounting purposes only.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes. We will only disclose your information if required to do so by law.

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics. The only browser storage used is localStorage to save your shopping cart between visits — this data never leaves your device.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information

To exercise any of these rights, please message us on WhatsApp at +1 (242) 455-1882.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of The Bahamas.
